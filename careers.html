Careers

Join a team that ships real products, not just proposals.

We're looking for developers and affiliate partners who want to build apps, platforms, and commerce systems with a small, accountable team. If you prefer shipping working software to writing buzzword-heavy proposals, we should talk.

Why build with us

Our work is grounded in products you can point to: Wimp-Drop, WimpSchool, WimpyBooks, and event booking systems. We value practical delivery, readable code, and teams that can maintain what they ship.

  • Meaningful product work, not agency scraps
  • Live launches, real user feedback, and constant iteration
  • Flexible roles for developers and affiliate/partner contributors

Developer

Front-end, back-end, and full-stack engineers who can own a shipped product and keep it stable after launch.

  • React, Vue, Node.js, or API integrations
  • Feature work, bug fixes, and deploy-ready releases
  • Product-focused code reviews and clean handoff docs

Affiliate/Partner

Designers, growth operators, or technical partners who want to collaborate on landing pages, web funnels, and product launches.

  • Brand systems that support product releases
  • Launch planning with a software delivery lens
  • Referral and affiliate relationships for high-value projects
